PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 23, 2026

In short: Laser Dodge has no sign-in and no account, and never asks for your name, e-mail, phone number or location. Your progress is stored on your device (and in your own iCloud or Google device backup). If you use the leaderboard, friend codes or online duel, a randomly generated identifier (never your name or email) is sent to our server. Ads are served only by Google AdMob; on Android every ad request is marked as child-directed and the advertising ID is never accessed. Purchases are processed by RevenueCat and the app stores.

1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy explains how the Laser Dodge mobile application ("the App") for iOS and Android handles user privacy. The App is developed by Mustafa Yalçın Canbay, Kayra Çolak, and Gökberk Dökmen ("the Developers", "we").

By downloading and using the App, you agree to this Policy.

2. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

We never ask for or collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, precise location, Apple ID or Google account. The App does not request or process:

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your precise location (GPS)
  • Contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone
  • Any custom analytics or behavioral tracking of our own
  • Free-text chat or user-written names — there is no chat, and leaderboard names are generated by our server

3. NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED

The App does not require registration or sign-in. There is no user account, and no login credentials are stored. Some features described below (leaderboard, friends, online duel) work through an anonymous, randomly generated identifier instead of an account — see Section 4.

4. LEADERBOARDS, FRIENDS AND ONLINE DUEL

To place you on the leaderboard the App generates a random identifier on your device and sends it to our server. On iOS this identifier is stored in the iOS Keychain and mirrored to your iCloud; on Android it is stored in the App's private storage, which is included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account. This lets a reinstall keep your place. The server never stores the identifier itself — only a SHA-256 hash of it. Your display name is generated by the server; you never type it.

When you set a personal record we send: the score, the game mode, how long the run lasted, the world it was played in, and which platform and store the app was installed from. If you use friend codes we store your friend list. During an online duel your opponent sees your generated name, your character, and your live score; the duel record is deleted from the server as soon as both players have seen the result.

This data is processed by Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Functions) in the European Union (europe-west1). As with any internet request, our server necessarily receives your device's IP address to send a response; we do not use it to identify you.

5. LOCAL GAME DATA AND DEVICE BACKUPS

Your in-game progress (records, Volts, unlocked characters and worlds, badges, settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not run any server that stores this progress. Your device's own backup may copy it to your personal cloud storage: on iOS the App backs up progress to your iCloud account so that a reinstall restores it; on Android the operating system's automatic backup may include it in your Google account backup. These backups are handled by Apple or Google under your own account, and we cannot access them. This is separate from the leaderboard/friends/duel data described in Section 4.

6. DELETING YOUR DATA

Open Settings → Ranking Data → "Delete my ranking data" in the App. This erases your name, friend code, friend list, and every score you have posted from our server. Your game itself — records, Volts, worlds, badges — is stored on your device and is not affected. To remove your local game data, uninstall the App; to remove backed-up copies, delete the App's data from your iCloud or Google account backup settings. You can also e-mail us (Section 14) and we will help.

7. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

The App is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. No other advertising network is used. Ads appear in two ways only:

  • A banner ad at the bottom of the screen. On Android it appears only on menu screens, inside a box labelled "AD".
  • Optional rewarded video ads that start only when you tap a button that clearly says you will watch an ad and what you get for it (continuing a run, or a Volt gift). Declining the offer never blocks the game.

To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google receives information such as your IP address (used to estimate your country or region), device and app information (for example device model, operating system, app version and language), and ad interaction data (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped).

On Android: the App does not access the Android advertising ID (the permission is removed from the App). Every ad request is marked for child-directed treatment, which turns off personalized ads, interest-based advertising and remarketing, and ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (G).

On iOS: the App asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Only if you allow it may AdMob use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalize ads. If you decline, the identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the App uses Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) to ask for consent where required. Where it applies, you can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → "Privacy options".

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

8. IN-APP PURCHASES

The App offers optional in-app purchases for in-game Volts. Volts can be used to unlock characters (skins) and worlds (themes) within the App. Volts have no real-world value, cannot be exchanged for money, are non-transferable, and are non-refundable except as required by the relevant app store policy. Volts earned from rewarded ads follow the same rules.

Purchases are managed by RevenueCat and processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated customer identifier, the details of your purchases, and basic device and app information so that purchases can be validated and restored. Your payment information is handled solely by Apple or Google and is never seen by us. See:

  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App relies on the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (with Google User Messaging Platform) — advertising and consent
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management
  • Google Firebase — leaderboard and online duel server (EU region)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — app distribution and payment processing
  • Apple iCloud / Android Backup — device backups under your own account

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else.

10. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Laser Dodge is a game for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13: the App never asks for a name, e-mail address, phone number, location or photo, has no chat, and leaderboard names are generated by our server. On Android, all ads are requested as child-directed, without the advertising ID, without personalization, and limited to general-audience (G) content. Parents can delete leaderboard data in the App (Section 6) or contact us at any time.

11. DATA SECURITY

We do not collect or store directly identifying personal data on any server. Leaderboard and duel data is limited to a hashed device identifier, a server-generated display name, and gameplay statistics, processed by Google Firebase in the EU over encrypted connections (HTTPS). All other gameplay data remains on your device and in your own device backups.

12. DATA RETENTION

Leaderboard and friend data is kept until you delete it in the App. Online duel records are deleted as soon as both players have seen the result. Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the App.

13. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App means you accept the current version.
